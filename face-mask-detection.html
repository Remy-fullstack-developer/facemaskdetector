HCI NOTES

  • Accuracy: varied samples (people, masks, angles, glasses) → better results.
  • Lighting: app warns when the face is too dark; train in the light you will use.
  • Feedback: colour border + text + gate status + optional voice/beep; confidence bars.
  • Uncertainty: low confidence shows “not sure” instead of a wrong decision; results are smoothed over time to avoid flicker.
  • Privacy: processing is local; frames are not stored or sent. Camera can be stopped any time.
